World map data notice

The country outlines in world-map.svg were derived from the geo-countries dataset maintained at https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries, which in turn sources Natural Earth country boundaries.

Natural Earth data is public domain. The datasets/geo-countries repository publishes the packaged data under the Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL).

The yellow network arcs and location markers are original illustrative UI elements. They communicate worldwide availability and do not represent live worker telemetry.
